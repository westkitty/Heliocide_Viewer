# CANON — StarSilk Continuity Constraints

This document formalizes the canonical lore rules and narrative constraints governing **Heliocide Observatory**.

---

## 1. The Blood Eclipse War & The Aureal Gate

- **Duration:** The Blood Eclipse War lasted 170 years between the **Drakken Empire** (united under Mother's planetary will) and the **Administration**.
- **The Event at Year 170:** At the final collapse of the Aureal Gate, the Drakken deployed **Starsilk as a weapon** for the first time in recorded history.
- **Significance:** This was the first empirical proof that the **Shard God** could be harmed.
- **The Response:** The Shard God responded immediately by initiating a surgical, disproportionate heliocide strike across the **Hal'Ven Cluster**, forcing stellar bodies into sequential gravitational collapse to seed singularities and form the **Siege Wall** around the Drakken territory.

---

## 2. Character & Psychological Authority: The Shard God

- **Naming Lock:** The character is strictly and exclusively named the **`Shard God`**. Legacy naming (such as `Tiger`) is obsolete and forbidden in runtime UI, code identifiers, comments, and narrative dialogue.
- **Motivations:** The heliocide is **not** emotional revenge or mindless fury. It is cold, pragmatic, strategic containment and self-preservation. The Drakken proved they had a mechanism to harm him; his calculation is that the entire sector must be collapsed into gravitational quarantine to prevent propagation of the threat.
- **Behavioral Tone:** Cold, deliberate, surgical, terrifyingly precise, calm, and free of melodramatic theatrics.
- **Visual Description:** Large humanoid/reptilian anatomy with matte black skin, glowing cyan crystal dorsal spines, crystalline mane, heavy tail, clawed appendages, cyan glowing veins and eyes, and pure cyan energy manifestations.

---

## 3. Starsilk & Starsilk Macros

- **Nature:** Starsilk is a literal, programmable cosmological substance discovered during ancient star-terraforming projects.
- **Starsilk Macros:** Repeatable action-loops embedded into the physical fabric of spacetime.
- **Administration Understanding:** The Administration does not understand Starsilk and possesses no ability to reverse or counter its deployment.

---

## 4. The Siege Wall — Visual & Interface Laws

- **Physical Sky Law:** To any observer in physical space (aboard a station, on a planet, or in an orbital ship), the Siege Wall **must NEVER appear as a geometric lattice, web, wireframe, or glowing grid**. It appears solely as a vast, terrifying, irregular **swath of starless blackness** expanding across the cosmos as stars are systematically erased.
- **Tactical Interface Distinction:** Diegetic tactical displays may represent the containment network with abstract nodes and connectivity vectors to explain strategic containment geometry, but this is explicitly an interface schematic, not physical space.

---

## 5. Distinction from Other Events

- **NOT The Starbinding:** The Starbinding is a distinct, later historical epoch involving billions of star dives. No Starbinding mechanics or terminology apply to this project.
- **Finality of Death:** Death in Starsilk continuity is absolute. There are no disembodied ghosts, trapped conscious souls in plasma, or afterlife data streams.

---

## 6. Moral Framing & Station Context

- The player is an unnamed Administration observer stationed aboard **Observation Station HV-88** in the Hal'Ven Cluster.
- The station and crew are mere collateral in a cosmic calculation.
- The catastrophe is deterministic and unpreventable.
- The post-catastrophe state concludes with the cold Administration status line: `CONTAINMENT ACHIEVED`.
